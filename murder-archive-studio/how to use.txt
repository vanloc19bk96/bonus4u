Murder Archive Studio
=====================

Double-click index.html. It opens in your web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari)
and works without the internet. Nothing to install.

Make "Thousands of suspects. A few clues. One killer." puzzle books for Amazon KDP:
the reader crosses out records one clue at a time until only the killer is left.

Eight kinds of book (step 1, "Book type"):
  - Lottery & raffle tickets   ticket number, time, table, price, payment, lucky numbers
  - Passenger manifest         name, occupation, class, cabin, port (ocean liner, night train,
                               riverboat, cruise ship, overnight flight)
  - Hotel guest register       entry, guest, arrival date, room, nights, rate
  - Staff roster               badge, name, department, role, floor, clock-in time
                               (hospital, museum, casino, country estate, film studio)
  - Guest list (names only)    up to 20,000 names; every clue is about the letters
  - Phone records              phone ID, time, call length, tower, number called
  - Coordinates archive        latitude and longitude, with an Atlas of real cities
  - Visitor log                museum, theme park, zoo or film festival scans
Each kind has several settings, plus "My own setting" for your own idea.

Four steps, on the left of the screen:
  1. Set up your book    - book type, pen name, setting, difficulty, size, number of records.
  2. Write the story     - copy one prompt into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini, paste the reply back.
  3. Design the cover    - pick one of 50 cover designs. Each thumbnail shows your own title,
                           pen name and real records from your case. "For your book" shows the
                           designs that suit your book type (ships, hotels, hospitals, museums,
                           phone records, maps...); the other buttons show the six groups:
                           Classic, Case file, Bold, Tickets & travel, Places, Noir & seasonal.
                           The front, spine and back are made for you at the exact size KDP asks for.
  4. Check and download  - the app checks every KDP print rule, then gives you two PDFs:
                           the interior (manuscript) and the cover.

What every book can include:
  - The archive told in chapters (by class, department, arrival date, hour or surname)
  - Clues that lead to exactly one record, with every clue needed (checked by the app)
  - A title, a story note and a one-line reading tip for every clue
  - Three short story interludes between the clues
  - The Practice Case: seven records and four clues, worked through, for first-time readers
  - Detective's Hints: two hints for every clue, never giving the answer away
  - Checkpoints: how many records should be left after each clue
  - The Verdict Cipher: readers unlock the killer's name with their answer, so they can check
    it with no spoilers
  - Cut-out clue cards (printed with a blank back), notes pages and a Certificate of Detection
  - Your Amazon listing: title, subtitle, description and 7 keywords, ready to copy

Click "New case" (step 1, More options) for every new book: each case file is an archive,
an answer and a set of clues no other book has.

"See a finished sample" shows two complete example books (a lottery register and a passenger
manifest). Project files saved by the Lottery Murder Book Maker open here too, unchanged.

If you have any questions, feel free to let me know.
