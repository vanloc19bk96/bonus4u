CROSS IT OUT PUZZLE PRINTS
==========================

Double-click index.html. It opens in your web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari)
and works without the internet. Nothing to install. The AI chat in step 2 is the only part
that needs the internet, and you open that yourself.

The first time it opens, type the access key that came with your purchase and click
"Open the app". It is remembered on that computer, so you only type it once.

1. Set up your book: title, pen name, readers, page layout and how many cases each level has.
2. Add your own puzzles with AI: click "Copy prompt", paste it into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini,
   then paste the whole reply back into the app and click "Add these packs". Each pack gives
   two cases. "Your prompts" shows how many prompts the book takes and which one is next
   (e.g. "Prompt 2 of 4"); click a number to see what an earlier prompt added.
3. Review the cases: look through them; "New clues" gives a case a new line-up and clues,
   "Remove" takes it out of the book.
4. Click "Book cover" at the top of the left panel (the panel and the preview switch to the cover), pick one of 40 cover templates (filter by Classic, Mystery, Playful, Seasonal or Cross-out), keep its own colors or pick another palette, and click "Download cover PDF".
5. Check and download: when every item is green, click "Download PDF" and upload it to KDP
   as the paperback manuscript (no bleed, black & white).

Click "Save project file" to keep your work; open it later with "Open project file".
Every case comes from your own AI packs, so your book is different from every other book
made with this app.

If you have any questions, feel free to let me know.
