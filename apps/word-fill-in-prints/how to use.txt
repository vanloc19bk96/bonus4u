Double-click index.html to get started. No internet connection or installation needed
(you only need the internet to use an AI chat such as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Grok
for your word lists).

1. Click "Add words".
   - Type your book topic, who it is for, how many puzzles you want and your themes
     (or click "Fill with random ideas").
   - The app splits the job into prompts of 10 lists each. Click "Copy" next to
     prompt 1, paste it into a new AI chat, paste the AI's answer in the box on the
     right and click "Add". Then copy prompt 2 into the SAME chat (so no theme or word
     repeats), paste the answer, click "Add", and so on until every prompt is done.
     If the AI stops early, type "continue" in the same chat and Add that part too.
   - Prefer your own words? Type them in the "Your word lists" box, a title line then its words:
       1. Breakfast Foods
       EGG, HAM, OAT, JAM, TOAST, JUICE, CEREAL, ...
     or one theme per line:  Farm Animals: cow, horse, sheep, goat, pig, donkey, ...
   - Click "Use these words".
2. Pick your trim size, difficulty and extra pages on the left. Every option already has
   a sensible default.
3. Click Generate, check the pages in the preview, then click Download PDF and upload
   it to KDP as your interior file.

Good to know
- Every puzzle is checked by a solver: it has exactly one solution.
- Every book gets brand-new grid layouts (random seed). To rebuild the exact same book,
  copy the seed shown under Advanced and type it in the Seed box.
- The KDP check in the bottom-left must be all green before you upload
  (at least 24 pages, text 7 pt or larger, everything inside the margins, fonts embedded).
- Your words and settings are NOT saved: reloading the page (F5) clears them. Use
  "Save words to a file" to keep a copy, then load it back next time.

If you have any questions, feel free to let me know.
