OCCUPATION TRIVIA BOOK MAKER
============================

Double-click index.html. It opens in your web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari)
and works without the internet. Nothing to install. ChatGPT in step 3 is the only part
that needs the internet, and you open that yourself.

1. The job: pick the job the book is about (nurse, teacher, firefighter, trucker,
   electrician ... 35 jobs), or "Jobs & careers of every kind", or "Another job" to type
   any job yourself. Pick who reads it (people in the job, students, or family and fans)
   and the country. Type your pen name. Leave the title empty to use the suggestion,
   or click one of the title ideas. "Big word on the cover" is the job word printed huge
   on the front (NURSE, TRUCKER ...).
2. Chapters and pages: pick the chapters (history, famous people, tools, lingo, pop
   culture ...), how many questions each chapter has, multiple choice only or mixed with
   true-or-false, where the answers go, the extra pages (Did You Know? fun facts, score
   card with ranks, stories from the job, certificate of achievement), the page size and
   the text size.
3. Write the questions with ChatGPT:
     - click "Copy prompt",
     - click "Open ChatGPT", paste (Ctrl+V) and press Enter,
     - when it has finished, click "Copy code" on its reply,
     - paste it into the box and click "Add to book".
   Do the same for every prompt ("Prompt 2 of 6" ...). Green numbers are done.
   The last prompt writes the welcome letter, the fun facts, the ranks and the back cover text.
   If a chapter is still short, copy its prompt again: it only asks for the missing questions.
   Claude, Gemini or Copilot work the same way.
4. Check and download: when every item is green, click "Download book PDF".

COVER: click "Book cover" at the top of the left panel (the panel and the preview switch to
the cover). Pick one of the 44 templates (filter them by group: On the job, Bold & bright,
Quiz & game, Elegant, Retro & gift). Each starts in its own colors; pick other colors if you
like. Check the words on the cover, then click "Download cover PDF".
Click "Book pages" to go back to the inside of the book.

Upload to KDP: paperback, black & white interior, no bleed, the same trim size you picked.
The book PDF is the manuscript; the cover PDF is "a cover you already have".
Download the cover again whenever the page count changes (the spine width follows it).

The app shuffles the answer choices so A, B, C and D are each right about as often.
Read the questions once before you publish: AI facts are usually right, but not always,
and rules, ranks and licenses differ between countries. Do not put brand names, logos or
the names of professional associations in your title.

One job, many books: keep the same chapters and change the job in step 1, or keep the job
and change the readers (students, family and fans) for a second edition.

The app does not keep your work in the browser: closing or reloading the tab clears it.
Click "Save project file" to keep your work; open it later with "Open project file".

If you have any questions, feel free to let me know.
