WORD TRAILS PUZZLE PRINTS
=========================

Double-click index.html. It opens in your web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari)
and works without the internet. Nothing to install. ChatGPT in step 3 is the only part
that needs the internet, and you open that yourself.

The book: a grid of letters with a playful title on each page. Hidden in the grid are theme
words that bend in any direction (up, down, sideways, diagonal) and one "span word" that names
the theme and runs from one edge to the opposite edge. Every letter is used exactly once.
Then come two rounds of hints and full solutions with the trails drawn in.

It is the same kind of puzzle as the Strands game. "Strands" and the newspaper's name belong to
their owner, so leave them off your title, subtitle, cover and keywords. The app calls the
puzzle "Word Trails"; you can choose your own name in step 1.

1. The book: who will solve it, the theme of the book, how many puzzles, the title and your pen name.
   Choosing Kids or Young kids also sets an easy level, a fitting grid and the "For kids" theme (you can
   change them). The prompts then ask for easy words, simple titles and child-friendly subjects, and
   the How to Play page uses simpler words. Read every puzzle yourself: the app checks the letters,
   not whether a child knows a word.
2. Puzzles and pages: the grid size (6 x 8 is the classic), the level, hints, puzzle log and
   stars, the page size, one or two puzzles to a page and the text size.
     Large print (14 pt) and Extra large (16 pt) keep every letter in the book at that size or
     bigger: labels, page numbers, hints, and the grid letters on the puzzle and solution pages
     (4 solutions to a page on 8.5 x 11). Many large print guides ask for 16 pt. If a grid is too
     big for a small page size, step 4 says so: pick a bigger page or a smaller grid.
     Easy: gentle trails; the page shows each word's first letter and length.
     Medium: trails turn more; the page shows each word's length.
     Hard: trails twist at every chance; the page only says how many words to find.
     Mixed: easy, then medium, then hard, each with its own opening page.
3. Write the puzzles with ChatGPT:
     - click "Copy prompt",
     - click "Open ChatGPT", paste (Ctrl+V) and press Enter,
     - when it has finished, click "Copy code" on its reply,
     - paste it into the box and click "Add to book".
   Do the same for every prompt ("Prompt 2 of 11" ...). Green ticks are done.
   ChatGPT only writes the titles, themes and words. It is asked for more words than a grid holds
   (14 for a 6 x 8 grid). The app picks the words whose letters fill the grid exactly, builds the
   trails, and proves that the puzzle has exactly one solution. A word that appears in the title
   is never used.
   A puzzle that does not fit as ChatGPT wrote it is fixed for you: a span word that is too long is
   shortened (WINTER BREAKFAST becomes BREAKFAST), the grid may hold a word or two more, and in the end
   one of the puzzle's own words can become the span word. Step 4 lists what was changed.
   If a puzzle still has no grid, copy its prompt again for more words. If you would rather not, the
   PDF leaves that puzzle out and numbers the rest without a gap, and the title page and cover say the
   real number. No page is ever blank. Use the same number in your KDP listing.
   The last prompt writes the welcome, the closing note and the back cover text.
   Claude, Gemini or Copilot work the same way.

   FIXING A PUZZLE (a weak word or a title you don't like):
     - Click "Edit" next to a puzzle: change the title, theme, span word or words right there.
       "New grid" gives a different layout of the letters.
     - Or let ChatGPT fix it: click "Copy check prompt". Paste ChatGPT's reply into the box as
       usual and click "Add to book". The app sees the puzzle already has words and asks:
       choose "Replace" to take the corrected version.
     - Or start over: click "Write these puzzles again", copy the prompt again and paste the reply.
4. Check and download: when every item is green, click "Download book PDF".

COVER: click "Book cover" at the top of the left panel (the panel and the preview switch to the
cover). Pick one of the 30 templates (Bright, Bold, Calm). Each starts in its own colors; pick
other colors if you like. Check the words on the cover, then click "Download cover PDF".
Click "Book pages" to go back to the inside of the book.

Upload to KDP: paperback, black & white interior, no bleed, the same trim size you picked.
The book PDF is the manuscript; the cover PDF is "a cover you already have".
Download the cover again whenever the page count changes (the spine width follows it).
KDP asks whether AI helped make the book: the themes and words came from an AI chat, so answer yes for the text.

Solve two or three puzzles yourself before you publish. The app proves each grid has one solution,
but only a reader can tell whether a theme feels fair.

The app does not keep your work in the browser: closing or reloading the tab clears it.
Click "Save project file" to keep your work; open it later with "Open project file".
