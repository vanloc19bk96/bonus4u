SLITHERLINK PUZZLE PRINTS
=========================

Double-click index.html. It opens in your web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari)
and works without the internet. Nothing to install.

1. Pick a Quick start preset (or set the grid size, number of puzzles and difficulty).
2. Click Create puzzles and wait for the green "Ready for KDP" status.
3. Click Download PDF and upload it to KDP as the paperback manuscript (no bleed, black & white).

What is in the book
- Title, copyright and contents pages.
- A How to Play page with an example puzzle and its solution.
- A Solving Tips page: 8 patterns with pictures (0s, 3s in corners, two 3s side by side, ...).
- The puzzles, each level (and each grid size of a mixed book) starting on a new page.
- Solutions with the loop drawn as one thick line and the inside shaded.
- A thank-you page.

Grid sizes: 5 x 5 up to 25 x 25, or "Mixed" sizes that grow from small to large grids inside
each level. Levels: Easy, Medium, Hard and Expert (5 x 5 has Easy and Medium, 7 x 7 has no Expert).

Every puzzle has exactly one solution: the app solves each one step by step with logic only,
and grids up to 10 x 10 are also checked by a second, brute-force solver.

Big grids take longer: a 25 x 25 Expert puzzle can take about 20 seconds, so a 100-puzzle
giant book needs a few minutes. You can keep working on the other settings meanwhile.

Tips
- Under Page you can turn on light guide lines between the dots, and choose whether the
  solutions show the numbers (turning them off fits more solutions on a page).
- The same Seed (under Advanced) always gives the same puzzles. Click New puzzles for a new set.

If you have any questions, feel free to let me know.
