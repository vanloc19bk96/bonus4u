COLOR BY LINE GENERATOR
=======================

Make a whole "Color By Line" coloring book for Amazon KDP: every picture is drawn with
colored outlines and white insides, and the app builds the print-ready interior PDF.

Double-click index.html (Chrome or Edge recommended). Nothing to install. Drawing the
pictures needs the internet; everything else works offline.


1. YOUR ACCESS KEY
   The first time, paste the access key you received and click "Save and continue".
   It is kept only in this browser on this computer. Change it any time with the key
   button at the top right.

2. SET UP THE BOOK (left side)
   - Your book: title, subtitle and your pen name (shown on the title and copyright pages).
   - Look of the pictures: who it is for, the art style, the line colors, line weight and
     detail. The defaults copy the best-selling Color By Line books: cozy style, natural
     colors, medium lines.
   - Print: book size (8.5 x 8.5 in is what most bestsellers use), the frame around each
     picture, and which extra pages to include.

3. ADD PICTURES
   Click "Add pictures":
   - Idea packs: 10 ready packs of 30 pictures each (Cozy Moments, Cute Animal Friends,
     Hello Autumn, Cute Halloween, Cozy Christmas, Spring Garden, Summer by the Sea,
     Little Shops and Houses, Sweet Treats, Flowers and Bouquets).
   - Paste my ideas: one picture per line.
   - Get ideas from an AI chat: copy the prompt, paste it into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini,
     then paste its list back.

4. DRAW
   Click "Draw ... pictures". Several pictures are drawn at the same time; you can keep
   working, and "Stop" takes the waiting ones off the list.
   - Click a picture to see it large, change its text, redraw it (old versions are kept,
     pick the one you like) or download it.
   - When one picture looks just right, click its star. Every new picture then matches its
     line weight, colors and detail, so the whole book looks drawn by one artist.
   - The quality check marks pictures with black lines, colored-in areas, a blank page or a
     single color. See them under "Needs a look" and redraw them.

5. COLOR GUIDE
   Buyers love a colored example of every picture at the back of the book. Click
   "Color guide" to make them (one extra image per picture).

6. EXPORT
   Click "Export book" and "Download book PDF". The PDF has the title page, copyright,
   "This book belongs to", how to color, a "Try your colors" page in the book's own line
   colors, every picture on its own right-hand page with a blank back (so markers do not
   bleed into the next picture), and the color guide. Fonts are embedded for KDP.
   On KDP choose: Paperback, Standard color interior (the lines are in color), white paper,
   No bleed, and the same trim size.
   "Download pictures (.zip)" saves every PNG if you want to build the book elsewhere.

7. SAVE YOUR WORK
   The book stays in this browser between visits. "Save project" makes one file with every
   picture inside; "Open project" loads it again, on any computer.

SETTINGS (gear icon)
   Image server (Global or China), drawing quality (Draft, Standard, Best), picture size
   (2K is about 280 DPI on 8.5 x 8.5 in; 4K is sharper but slower), how many pictures to
   draw at the same time, and "make the paper pure white".
