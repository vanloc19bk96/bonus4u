ESCAPE ROOM BOOK MAKER
======================

Double-click index.html. It opens in your web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari)
and works without the internet. Nothing to install. ChatGPT in step 3 is the only part
that needs the internet, and you open that yourself.

The book: a story in locked rooms, played with only a pencil. No QR codes, no app, no scissors.
Each room holds 3 or 4 puzzles and every puzzle gives one digit. The digits make the room's door
code. The reader looks the code up in the door's table and turns to the page it names: the next
room if it is right, a Dead End page if it is wrong. A Dead End says which puzzle to look at again
and sends the reader back. The rooms are shuffled through the book, so only the right code gets
you there.

Who does what:
  ChatGPT writes the story: the rooms, the opening and ending, and the words a puzzle shows.
  The app makes all the rest and proves it: the 18 kinds of puzzle, three hints and a full answer
  for each, the code tables, and every page number. It plays the finished book from the first
  room to the ending before you download it.

1. The book: who plays (adults, teens and families, or kids 8 to 12), where it happens, how many
   rooms, how it ends (a final word from key letters, or naming the culprit), the title and your
   pen name. Choosing Kids also sets the easy level and the gentler puzzle types.
2. Puzzles and pages: the level (Rising is easy rooms first and hard rooms last), 3 or 4 puzzles a
   room, which puzzle types are in, and the page size and text size. 6 x 9 in is what most escape
   room books use.
3. Write the story with ChatGPT:
     - click "Copy prompt",
     - click "Open ChatGPT", paste (Ctrl+V) and press Enter,
     - when it has finished, click "Copy code" on its reply,
     - paste it into the box and click "Add to book".
   Do the first prompt first: it writes the story the others build on. Then do every other prompt.
   Green ticks are done. Copying a prompt again only asks for what is still missing or could not be
   used, such as a message that is too long. Claude, Gemini or Copilot work the same way.

   FIXING A ROOM:
     - Click "Edit" next to a room: change any text, change a puzzle's type, or click "New version
       of this puzzle" for a different one. The window shows the digit, the hints and the answer.
     - Or let ChatGPT edit it: click "Copy check prompt", and paste its reply in the usual box. A
       reply replaces what it sends back and leaves the rest alone.
     - Or start over: click "Write these rooms again", copy the prompt again and paste the reply.
     - "Edit the story" holds the opening, final word, suspects, ending and back cover.
4. Check and download: when every item is green, click "Download book PDF".

COVER: click "Book cover" at the top of the left panel. Pick one of the templates (Settings, Locks
and keys, For kids). Each starts in its own colors; pick other colors if you like. Check the words
on the cover, then click "Download cover PDF". Click "Book pages" to go back.

Upload to KDP: paperback, black & white interior, no bleed, the same trim size you picked.
The book PDF is the manuscript; the cover PDF is "a cover you already have".
Download the cover again whenever the page count changes (the spine width follows it).
KDP asks whether AI helped make the book: the story came from an AI chat, so answer yes for the text.

Before you publish, play at least one room yourself with a pencil. The app proves the puzzles and the
route. It can't prove a riddle (ChatGPT writes those, so read each one), and only a reader can tell
whether a story feels fair. To leave riddles out, untick them in step 2.

Editing in Canva: "Book for Canva (.pptx)" in step 4 gives you editable pages. Change the words, not
the order: the page numbers in the tables are plain text, so moving pages breaks the route.

Save your work with "Save project file" (bottom left) and open it again later. Nothing is stored in
the browser, so save before you close the tab.

Fonts: Source Serif 4 and Source Sans 3 (SIL Open Font License, see the fonts folder), embedded in the PDF.
