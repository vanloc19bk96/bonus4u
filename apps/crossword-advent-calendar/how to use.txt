CROSSWORD ADVENT CALENDAR MAKER
===============================

Double-click index.html. It opens in your web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari)
and works without the internet. Nothing to install. ChatGPT in step 3 is the only part
that needs the internet, and you open that yourself.

The book: one crossword a day from December 1, each on its own two-page spread (the grid
on the left page, the clues on the right). One square in every grid has a thick border:
its letter goes on the letter strip at the front, and on the last day the strip spells a
hidden sentence. Week pages with Advent candles, a memory question a day and all the
solutions at the back, like the bestselling crossword advent calendars on Amazon.

1. The book: who will solve it, the theme of the words, the countdown (24 days Dec 1-24, 25 days Dec 1-25, 12 days Dec 13-24, or any other length from 7 to 24 days that ends on Christmas Eve)
   and, if you like, the Twelve Days bonus (12 more grids from December 26 to January 6).
   Type the hidden sentence or click an idea: it must have exactly one letter per day
   (the counter turns green). Set it BEFORE you copy the prompts, because each day's words
   are asked to include that day's letter. Type your pen name.
2. Puzzles and pages: the grid size (13 x 13 is the bestseller's), the easy start (answers
   printed in the first grids), difficulty stars (off unless you tick them: one star means
   three answers are printed, three stars none), the story line and memory question, the page size and the
   text size (large print suits older solvers).
3. Write the words with ChatGPT:
     - click "Copy prompt",
     - click "Open ChatGPT", paste (Ctrl+V) and press Enter,
     - when it has finished, click "Copy code" on its reply,
     - paste it into the box and click "Add to book".
   Do the same for every prompt ("Prompt 2 of 7" ...). Green ticks are done.
   ChatGPT only writes the words, clues and little texts: the app builds every grid itself
   and checks it (every answer crosses another, no stray words hide in the grid).
   ChatGPT is asked for more words than a grid holds (18 for 9x9, 24 for 11x11, 30 for 13x13,
   36 for 15x15); the app keeps the ones that cross best, so a puzzle has about 10, 15, 18
   or 22 answers. If a day's grid comes out small, copy its prompt again: it asks for a few more words.
   Click "View" next to a day to see its grid, remove a word you don't like, or click
   "New grid" for a different layout of the same words.
   The last prompt writes the week pages, the Christmas Eve note and the back cover text.
   Claude, Gemini or Copilot work the same way.
4. Check and download: when every item is green, click "Download book PDF".

COVER: click "Book cover" at the top of the left panel (the panel and the preview switch to
the cover). Pick one of the 30 templates (Cozy, Classic, Festive). Each starts in its own
colors; pick other colors if you like. Check the words on the cover, then click
"Download cover PDF". Click "Book pages" to go back to the inside of the book.

Upload to KDP: paperback, black & white interior, no bleed, the same trim size you picked.
The book PDF is the manuscript; the cover PDF is "a cover you already have".
Download the cover again whenever the page count changes (the spine width follows it).

Solve two or three grids yourself before you publish: AI clues are usually right, but not always.

The app does not keep your work in the browser: closing or reloading the tab clears it.
Click "Save project file" to keep your work; open it later with "Open project file".

If you have any questions, feel free to let me know.
