CRYPTOGRAM PRINTS
=================

Double-click index.html. It opens in your web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari)
and works without the internet. Nothing to install. ChatGPT in step 3 is the only part
that needs the internet, and you open that yourself.
(If you still have a shortcut to cryptogram-prints.html, it simply opens index.html.)

The book: each puzzle is a sentence (a famous quote, a Bible verse, a fun fact, a joke...)
written in a secret code: every letter is swapped for another letter, or for a number from
1 to 26. The solver cracks the code one letter at a time. Under each puzzle there is a line
for every letter, and a letter tracker with a box for each code. At the back come the hints
(one more letter for each puzzle) and the full solutions.

1. The book: who will solve it (adults, the whole family, or kids 8 to 12), what the hidden
   sentences are (famous quotes, inspiring quotes, funny quotes, proverbs, Bible verses,
   fun facts, jokes and riddles, holidays, for kids, or your own theme), how many puzzles
   (10 to 500), the title and your pen name.
2. Puzzles and pages: the level, the code (letters or numbers), how the name under a quote
   is shown, what goes in the book, the page size, puzzles a page (1 to 4) and the text size.
     Easy: short sentences, three letters given (four for kids).
     Medium: longer sentences, two letters given.
     Hard: the longest sentences, no letters given.
     Mixed: easy, then medium, then hard, each with its own opening page.
   The name under a quote can be in code with the quote (part of the puzzle), shown plainly
   under it (a clue), or only in the solutions. Bible references are always shown plainly.
   Text size: Regular (12 pt), Easy to read (14 pt) or Large print (16 pt).
   Only 16 pt is called "Large Print" on the title page and cover: that is the size KDP counts
   as large print. 14 and 16 pt keep every letter in the book at that size or bigger.
   Step 2 shows how many characters fit each puzzle at your settings. If you ask for more
   puzzles a page than fit, the app uses fewer and says so.
3. Write the puzzles. Two ways, and you can mix them:
     a) Quick start (famous quotes, inspiring quotes, proverbs): click "Fill from the library".
        The app has 180 famous quotes and proverbs from public-domain books, each checked
        against its source. A big book needs more than the library has: write the rest with
        ChatGPT.
     b) ChatGPT:
        - click "Copy prompt",
        - click "Open ChatGPT", paste (Ctrl+V) and press Enter,
        - when it has finished, click "Copy code" on its reply,
        - paste it into the box and click "Add to book".
        Do the same for every prompt ("Prompt 2 of 5" ...). Green ticks are done.
   Each prompt asks for 25 puzzles, and for sentences no longer than the room a puzzle has on
   the page at your settings, so whatever comes back fits. The app makes a new code for every
   puzzle (no letter ever stands for itself), picks the given letters and the hint, and lays
   out the pages. A sentence a little too long simply gets a page of its own.
   If ChatGPT leaves a puzzle out, copy the same prompt again: it now asks only for what is
   missing. A reply that numbers the next batch from 1 again still lands in the right puzzles.
   A plain list of quotes, one a line ("quote | name"), can be pasted too.
   The last prompt writes the welcome, the closing note and the back cover text. You can change
   the welcome and closing notes in the boxes under "Your puzzles".
   Claude, Gemini or Copilot work the same way.

   CHECK THE QUOTES: chat models sometimes put a famous name on a line that person never said.
   The prompts tell ChatGPT to use only real quotes it is sure of, and the check prompt asks it
   to check every quote and name again. Look up any quote you are unsure of before you publish.

   FIXING A PUZZLE:
     - Click "Edit" next to a puzzle: change the sentence or the name right there. You see the
       coded sentence as the page prints it. "New code" gives it a different code.
     - Or let ChatGPT check it: click "Copy check prompt", paste the reply as usual and choose
       "Replace".
     - Or start over: click "Write these puzzles again", copy the prompt again and paste the reply.
   A puzzle that is not ready (no sentence, fewer than 15 letters, or far too long for a page) is
   left out of the PDF and the rest are numbered without a gap; the title page and cover say the
   real number. No page is ever blank. Use the same number on KDP.
4. Check and download: when every item is green, click "Download book PDF".
   "Book for Canva (.pptx)" gives an editable copy of the same pages for Canva.

COVER: click "Book cover" at the top of the left panel. Pick one of the 19 templates (Bright,
Bold, Calm). Each starts in its own colors; pick other colors if you like. Check the words on
the cover, then click "Download cover PDF". The coded phrase on the cover is decoration and
never comes from your book. Click "Book pages" to go back to the inside of the book.

Upload to KDP: paperback, black & white interior, no bleed, the same trim size you picked.
The book PDF is the manuscript; the cover PDF is "a cover you already have".
Download the cover again whenever the page count changes (the spine width follows it).
KDP asks whether AI helped make the book: if ChatGPT wrote the sentences, answer yes for the
text. Quotes from the app's library are public domain and were not written by AI.

The app does not keep your work in the browser: closing or reloading the tab clears it.
Click "Save project file" to keep your work; open it later with "Open project file".
A settings file saved by the first version of Cryptogram Prints opens too: its settings come
across and its puzzles are filled from the library.
