============= NAZO BOX LISTING LAUNCHPAD =============

Open the link in your bonus delivery, or double-click index.html (Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox).
Paste the activate key from your bonus delivery and click "Unlock the Launchpad".

1. Describe the book you built in NAZO BOX (who it's for, difficulty, what's inside)
2. Pick your 7 backend keywords ("Suggest 7 for me" or "Ask AI to pick 7")
3. Generate your title, subtitle, description and back cover text, check the Amazon rules list, then copy everything into KDP

Tip: rename your book in NAZO BOX to the title you choose and export the cover again, so the cover matches your KDP listing.
