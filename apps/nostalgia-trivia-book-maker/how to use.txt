NOSTALGIA TRIVIA BOOK MAKER
===========================

Double-click index.html. It opens in your web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari)
and works without the internet. Nothing to install. ChatGPT in step 3 is the only part
that needs the internet, and you open that yourself.

The books it makes look like the bestselling nostalgia trivia books on Amazon: one part per
decade (1940s to 2000s), each with an opener page (a short intro, the decade "at a glance"
year by year, and fun facts), themed rounds of multiple-choice questions, a "Remember When?"
page to write memories on, and the answers with a fun fact for every question.

1. Decades and title: tick the decades the book visits (or click a quick pick such as
   "1950s to 1990s"). Pick who reads it (seniors, adults who lived it, or the whole family)
   and the country they grew up in: the music, TV, brands and prices follow it. Type your
   pen name. Leave the title empty to use the suggestion, or click one of the title ideas.
   "Big word on the cover" is printed huge on the front (NOSTALGIA, RETRO, 1980s ...).
2. Rounds and pages: tick the rounds every decade has (Music & Dance, At the Movies,
   TV & Radio, Toys, Food, Everyday Life, Cars, Prices ... 15 themes), how many questions
   each round has, multiple choice only or mixed with true-or-false, where the answers go
   (after each decade or all at the back), the extra pages (decade openers, Remember When?
   pages, "This book belongs to" gift page, score card with ranks, certificate), the page
   size and the text size.
   "Total questions in the book": pick 300, 500, 600 ... and the app works out how many
   questions each round gets (every round gets the same number, so 500 over 30 rounds
   becomes 17 each = 510). Pick "Pick per round myself" to set the number per round by hand.
3. Write the questions with ChatGPT:
     - click "Copy prompt",
     - click "Open ChatGPT", paste (Ctrl+V) and press Enter,
     - when it has finished, click "Copy code" on its reply,
     - paste it into the box and click "Add to book".
   Do the same for every prompt ("Prompt 2 of 12" ...). Green numbers are done.
   Each decade's questions come first, then the "Decade pages" prompt (intro, at a glance,
   fun facts and memory prompts for every decade), then the last prompt (welcome letter,
   ranks and back cover text).
   If a round is still short, copy its prompt again: it only asks for the missing questions.
   Claude, Gemini or Copilot work the same way.
4. Check and download: when every item is green, click "Download book PDF".

COVER: click "Book cover" at the top of the left panel (the panel and the preview switch to
the cover). Pick one of the 44 templates (filter them by group: Retro, Bold & bright,
Quiz & game, Elegant, Memory lane). Each starts in its own colors; pick other colors if you
like. Check the words on the cover, then click "Download cover PDF".
Click "Book pages" to go back to the inside of the book.

Upload to KDP: paperback, black & white interior, no bleed, the same trim size you picked.
The book PDF is the manuscript; the cover PDF is "a cover you already have".
Download the cover again whenever the page count changes (the spine width follows it).

Large print: KDP lets you call a book "Large Print" only when the text is 16 pt or bigger.
The app starts at 8.5 x 11 in with 16 pt text, the size most senior trivia books use.

The app shuffles the answer choices so A, B, C and D are each right about as often.
Read the questions once before you publish: AI facts are usually right, but not always,
and years and prices are easy to mix up. Do not put band, show, movie, toy or brand names,
or celebrity names, in your title or keywords.

One app, many books: change the decades (a 1980s & 1990s book, a 1950s to 1970s book for
boomers), the readers (seniors, the whole family) or the country (United Kingdom, Canada,
Australia) for a whole series.

The app does not keep your work in the browser: closing or reloading the tab clears it.
Click "Save project file" to keep your work; open it later with "Open project file".
