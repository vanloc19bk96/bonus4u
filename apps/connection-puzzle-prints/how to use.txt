CONNECTION PUZZLE PRINTS
========================

Double-click index.html. It opens in your web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari)
and works without the internet. Nothing to install. ChatGPT in step 3 is the only part
that needs the internet, and you open that yourself.

The book: each puzzle is a board of 16 words. The solver sorts them into four groups of four
words that share a link (Card games, ___ fish, Hidden animal...). Some words seem to fit two
groups: those are the traps. Every page has a little circle on each tile for pencil marks and
lines to write the groups. At the back come the hints (the four links of each puzzle, not which
words go where) and full solutions.

It is the same kind of puzzle as the newspaper game "Connections". That name and the newspaper's
name belong to their owner, so leave them off your title, subtitle, cover and keywords. The app
calls the puzzle "Word Links"; you can choose your own name in step 1.

1. The book: who will solve it (adults, the whole family, or kids 8 to 12), the theme of the book,
   how many puzzles (10 to 500), the title and your pen name.
2. Puzzles and pages: the level, hints, puzzle log and stars, the page size, one or two puzzles
   a page and the text size.
     Easy: plain links and one gentle trap.
     Medium: one group plays with words, and a few words fit more than one group.
     Hard: two groups play with words, and traps are everywhere.
     Mixed: easy, then medium, then hard, each with its own opening page.
   One puzzle a page gives big tiles and a box for every answer. Two a page gives one writing line
   per group; it is only used when the tiles stay big enough.
   Text size: Regular (12 pt), Easy to read (14 pt) or Large print (16 pt, the default).
   Only 16 pt is called "Large Print" on the title page and cover: that is the size KDP counts as
   large print, so tick KDP's Large Print box only with this setting. 14 and 16 pt keep every letter
   in the book (hints and solutions too) at that size or bigger.
   A tile is narrow, so the bigger the text, the shorter the words that fit: step 2 shows how many
   letters fit, and the prompts ask ChatGPT for answers that short. For large print, 8.5 x 11 in
   is the best page size.
3. Write the puzzles with ChatGPT:
     - click "Copy prompt",
     - click "Open ChatGPT", paste (Ctrl+V) and press Enter,
     - when it has finished, click "Copy code" on its reply,
     - paste it into the box and click "Add to book".
   Do the same for every prompt ("Prompt 2 of 11" ...). Green ticks are done.
   ChatGPT writes the four groups of each puzzle, the most direct link first, and names its traps.
   The worked example on the How to Play page and the groups on the cover are picked from the app's
   own sets so that they share as little as possible with your puzzles: they never give a group away.
   The app checks every puzzle: four groups of four, 16 different answers, every answer short
   enough for its tile. It shuffles each board so no row or column gives a group away.
   It also warns ("Worth a look") when a link names its own answer (FISH under "Kinds of fish") or
   two answers are forms of one word (CAT and CATS), and when a link is used twice in the book.
   A puzzle that is not ready is left out of the PDF and the rest are numbered without a gap; the
   title page and cover say the real number. No page is ever blank. Use the same number on KDP.
   The last prompt writes the welcome, the closing note and the back cover text.
   You can change the welcome and closing notes in the boxes under "Your puzzles" (and the back
   cover text on the cover side), for example when ChatGPT promised something the book does not have.
   Claude, Gemini or Copilot work the same way.

   FIXING A PUZZLE:
     - Click "Edit" next to a puzzle: change any link or answer right there. The board shows each
       group in its own color. "Shuffle the board" gives a new order of the tiles.
     - Or let ChatGPT fix it: click "Copy check prompt". ChatGPT tries to sort each board another
       way and sends the puzzles back corrected. Paste the reply as usual and choose "Replace".
     - Or start over: click "Write these puzzles again", copy the prompt again and paste the reply.
4. Check and download: when every item is green, click "Download book PDF".
   "Book for Canva (.pptx)" gives an editable copy of the same pages for Canva.

COVER: click "Book cover" at the top of the left panel (the panel and the preview switch to the
cover). Pick one of the 19 templates (Bright, Bold, Calm). Each starts in its own colors; pick
other colors if you like. Check the words on the cover, then click "Download cover PDF".
The groups on the cover are decoration and never come from your book.
Click "Book pages" to go back to the inside of the book.

Upload to KDP: paperback, black & white interior, no bleed, the same trim size you picked.
The book PDF is the manuscript; the cover PDF is "a cover you already have".
Download the cover again whenever the page count changes (the spine width follows it).
KDP asks whether AI helped make the book: the puzzles came from an AI chat, so answer yes for the text.

Solve a few puzzles from each level before you publish. The app checks every board, but only a
reader can tell whether a link feels fair and a trap does not open a second way to sort.

The app does not keep your work in the browser: closing or reloading the tab clears it.
Click "Save project file" to keep your work; open it later with "Open project file".

If you have any questions, feel free to let me know.
