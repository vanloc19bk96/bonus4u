BIRTHDAY TRIVIA BOOK MAKER
==========================

Double-click index.html. It opens in your web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari)
and works without the internet. Nothing to install. ChatGPT in step 3 is the only part
that needs the internet, and you open that yourself.

The first time it opens, type the access key that came with your purchase and click
"Open the app". It is remembered on that computer, so you only type it once.

1. The birthday: pick the age (50th, 60th ... 100th or any age), the year of the birthday
   and the country they grew up in. Type your pen name. Leave the title empty to use the
   suggestion, or click one of the title ideas.
2. Chapters and pages: choose chapters by topic (music, movies, sports, prices ...) or by
   decade, how many questions each chapter has, where the answers go, the extra pages
   (the year you were born, Memory Lane, score card, birthday wishes), the page size and
   the text size (large print is best for older readers).
3. Write the questions with ChatGPT:
     - click "Copy prompt",
     - click "Open ChatGPT", paste (Ctrl+V) and press Enter,
     - when it has finished, click "Copy code" on its reply,
     - paste it into the box and click "Add to book".
   Do the same for every prompt ("Prompt 2 of 6" ...). Green numbers are done.
   The last prompt writes the welcome letter, the fun facts and the back cover text.
   If a chapter is still short, copy its prompt again: it only asks for the missing questions.
   Claude, Gemini or Copilot work the same way.
4. Check and download: when every item is green, click "Download book PDF".

COVER: click "Book cover" at the top of the left panel (the panel and the preview switch to
the cover). Pick one of the 50 templates (filter them by group: Bold & bright, Retro,
Elegant, Party, Memory lane). Each starts in its own colors; pick other colors if you like.
Check the words on the cover, then click
"Download cover PDF". Click "Book pages" to go back to the inside of the book.

Upload to KDP: paperback, black & white interior, no bleed, the same trim size you picked.
The book PDF is the manuscript; the cover PDF is "a cover you already have".
Download the cover again whenever the page count changes (the spine width follows it).

The app shuffles the answer choices so A, B, C and D are each right about as often.
Read the questions once before you publish: AI facts are usually right, but not always.

Click "Save project file" to keep your work; open it later with "Open project file".

If you have any questions, feel free to let me know.
