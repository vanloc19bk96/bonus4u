JIGSAW SUDOKU BOOK MAKER
========================

Double-click index.html. It opens in your web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari)
and works without the internet. Nothing to install.

1. Pick a Quick start preset (or set the grid size, puzzle type, number of puzzles and difficulty).
2. Click Create puzzles and wait for the green "Ready for KDP" status.
3. Click Download PDF and upload it to KDP as the paperback manuscript (no bleed, black & white).

The same Seed (under Advanced) always gives the same puzzles.

If you have any questions, feel free to let me know.
